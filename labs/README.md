# Labs

Type-2 lab notebooks — one per chapter (`lab01`–`lab14`), the design clinic
(`lab_design_clinic_no_single_best/`, a synthesis exercise sequenced after Chapter
11), and `lab16_capstone_scaffold` (a planning worksheet, not code). Chapter 15 has
no standalone lab — deployment thinking is exercised as a capstone reflection
instead.

**Formative — nothing to submit.** Each lab opens with a biomedical question and a
handful of `# TODO` cells (they `raise NotImplementedError` until filled in), and
closes with a sanity-check cell and a reflection prompt. Fill in the TODOs, rerun
top to bottom, and use the reflection cell for your own practice.

Every lab carries three badges (Colab / View / JupyterLite) in its second cell.
Browse all of them, alongside the book demo notebooks and capstone tracks, in the
[notebook gallery](https://farhad-abtahi.github.io/CM2013/).
